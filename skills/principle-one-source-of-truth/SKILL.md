# One source of truth

When chat, memory, and files disagree, **`docs/helm-spec.md` wins** after you update it.

Do not maintain parallel architecture/task pyramids. Disposable plans may exist in a single chat turn; they are not a second truth.

Steer phrase: `apply one-source-of-truth`
