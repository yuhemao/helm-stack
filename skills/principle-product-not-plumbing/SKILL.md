# Product not plumbing

Lead with what a user can do, what fails acceptance, and what needs a **human** product/UX call.

Hide file-by-file tours unless asked. Engineering decisions still happen — via **model gates** — and may appear as one-liners under **Handled by models** in `plain-status`, not as questions for the operator.

Steer phrase: `apply product-not-plumbing`
